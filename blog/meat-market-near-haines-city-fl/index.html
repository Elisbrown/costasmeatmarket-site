Looking for a Meat Market Near Haines City? Here's What You'll Find at Costa's

If you live in Haines City and you've been searching for a real meat market, we're right next door in Davenport. Here's what we carry, how to find us, and how to save when you shop.

Where we are

Costa's Meat Market is at 2169 Davenport Blvd, Davenport, FL 33837, inside Webb's Town Center. Davenport borders Haines City, so for most Haines City neighborhoods we're a short drive away. Look for the Costa's flag out front.

Tap Get directions to open the route on your phone, or call the counter at (863) 422-2313 if you need help finding us.

What's in the case

We're a full-service butcher counter, not a pre-packed meat aisle. On a typical day the case looks like this:

  • Beef: picanha, maminha (tri-tip), alcatra, coxão mole, ribeye, NY strip, skirt steak (churrasco), short ribs and costela, plus carne moída de primeira, our fresh ground beef.
  • Pork and chicken: pork chops, pork belly, whole chickens and wings.
  • Ready to cook: house-made linguiça, seasoned picadinho, marinated pork and seasoned chicken wings. See our guide to our ready-to-cook meats.
  • Groceries: Brazilian and Latin pantry staples, charcoal, and coarse churrasco salt.

Availability changes from day to day. If you're coming for something specific, call ahead or check our WhatsApp channel for today's arrivals.

Custom cuts and special orders

Tell us what you're cooking and we'll cut it for you: thick steaks for the grill, thin bistec, a whole picanha with the fat cap on, or a roast tied for the oven. For big parties or hard-to-find cuts, call a few days ahead so we can set it aside.

Not sure what your cut is called here? Our Latin and Brazilian cut translation guide has the English names.

Order ahead and skip the wait

Busy week? Place your order in our online store and pick it up at the counter. It's the easiest way to grab dinner on the way home from work.

How Haines City customers save

  • WhatsApp VIP group: discount codes, weekly specials and fresh arrivals, posted there first.
  • 5% off for following us: follow and engage with us on all of our socials and get 5% off your next purchase. Just show us at the counter (one coupon per customer).
  • Family bundles: several cuts put together so a week of dinners costs less.

All the details are in our coupons and deals guide.

Also close to you

Besides Haines City, we serve shoppers from ChampionsGate, Four Corners, Poinciana, Winter Haven and Kissimmee, plus visitors grilling at vacation homes near the parks. If that's you, read our vacation rental grilling guide.

Quick answers

Is there a meat market near Haines City, FL?

Yes. Costa's Meat Market is a full-service meat market and butcher shop in Davenport, which borders Haines City. We're at 2169 Davenport Blvd in Webb's Town Center.

Can I buy picanha near Haines City?

Yes. Picanha is one of our specialties at Costa's Meat Market in Davenport. Availability changes daily, so call (863) 422-2313 to check before you come.

Can I order meat online for pickup?

Yes. Order through the Costa's Meat Market online store and pick up your order at the counter in Davenport.
