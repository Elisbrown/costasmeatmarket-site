Meat Temperature Chart: Steak Doneness, Pork, Chicken and Ground Meat (°F and °C)

A thermometer is the cheapest tool that makes the biggest difference in your cooking. Here are the numbers we use behind the counter, in Fahrenheit and Celsius, plus how to take an accurate reading.

Medium135–140 °F (57–60 °C)140–145 °F (60–63 °C)Pink center

The USDA's safe minimum for whole cuts of beef, pork, lamb and veal is 145 °F (63 °C) with a 3-minute rest. Many people choose to eat steaks rarer than that; it's your call.

When tender beats "done"

Tough, collagen-rich cuts are safe long before they're good. Cook these to the higher numbers so the connective tissue melts:

  • Pot roast, short ribs, oxtail, brisket: about 195–205 °F (90–96 °C). See our pot roast and oxtail recipes.
  • Pulled pork and pernil: about 190–200 °F (88–93 °C). Here's our pernil recipe.
  • Pork belly: about 190–200 °F for tender, rendered layers.

Carryover cooking

Meat keeps cooking after it leaves the heat. Thick steaks and roasts can climb another 3 to 10 °F while they rest, so pull them early. Bigger roasts and hotter cooking methods mean more carryover.

How to use a meat thermometer

  1. Insert the probe into the thickest part, away from bone and fat.
  2. For thin steaks and chops, go in from the side so the tip sits in the center.
  3. Check a couple of spots on big roasts and whole chickens (the thigh, not touching the bone).
  4. Test it: in a glass of ice water it should read 32 °F (0 °C). If not, recalibrate or replace it.

Quick answers

What temperature is medium-rare steak?

About 130 to 135 °F (54 to 57 °C) after resting. Pull it from the heat around 125 to 130 °F, because the temperature rises as it rests.

What is the safe internal temperature for chicken?

165 °F (74 °C) for whole chicken, pieces and ground chicken. Wings and thighs are juicier and more tender at 175 to 185 °F.

What temperature should pork be cooked to?

Pork chops and roasts are safe at 145 °F (63 °C) with a 3-minute rest. Ground pork and fresh sausage should reach 160 °F (71 °C).
