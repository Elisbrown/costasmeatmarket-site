Feijoada: The Brazilian Black Bean Stew, and Which Meats to Buy in the U.S.

Feijoada is Brazil's Saturday feast: black beans slowly simmered with smoked sausage, pork and dried beef until everything turns rich and dark. Finding the right meats in the U.S. is half the battle. Here's the shopping list and the method.

The prep that matters: desalting

Salted meats like carne seca and salted pork ribs must soak before cooking or the feijoada will be far too salty. Cut them into large pieces, cover with cold water and refrigerate for 12 to 24 hours, changing the water 3 or 4 times. Soak the dried beans overnight too.

Feijoada (Brazilian black bean and pork stew)

  • Serves 8 servings
  • Prep 30 min
  • Cook 3 hr
  • Total 27 hr 30 min

Ingredients

  • 1 lb (450 g) dried black beans, soaked overnight
  • 1 lb (450 g) carne seca (dried salted beef), desalted 12 to 24 hours
  • 1 lb (450 g) pork ribs, cut between the bones
  • 1 lb (450 g) pork shoulder or loin, cut into large chunks
  • 8 oz (225 g) smoked sausage (calabresa or paio), sliced
  • 8 oz (225 g) fresh linguiça
  • 4 oz (115 g) bacon, diced
  • 3 bay leaves
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 1 large onion, chopped
  • 6 cloves garlic, minced
  • Salt and black pepper, to taste

Instructions

  1. The day before, soak the beans in plenty of water and soak the carne seca in cold water in the fridge, changing the water 3 or 4 times.
  2. Drain the beans and put them in a large pot with the bay leaves and water to cover by 2 inches. Simmer for 1 hour.
  3. Meanwhile, cut the carne seca into chunks and boil it in fresh water for 10 minutes; drain. In a skillet, brown the bacon, then the linguiça, pork ribs and pork chunks in batches.
  4. Add the carne seca, ribs and pork chunks to the beans. Simmer for 1 hour, adding hot water as needed to keep everything covered.
  5. Add the smoked sausage and the browned linguiça (sliced) and simmer 45 minutes to 1 hour more, until the beans are creamy and the meats are tender.
  6. In the skillet, heat the oil and cook the onion until golden, then the garlic for 1 minute. Add 2 ladles of beans, mash them, and stir this mixture back into the pot to thicken it.
  7. Simmer 10 more minutes, taste for salt (it may need very little) and pepper, and remove the bay leaves.
  8. Serve with white rice, couve, farofa, orange slices and torresmo.

The classic sides

  • White rice
  • Couve: collard greens sliced paper-thin and sautéed fast with garlic
  • Farofa (toasted cassava flour)
  • Orange slices, which cut the richness
  • Torresmo (crispy pork belly). Here's how to make it.
  • Hot pepper sauce on the side

Shortcut: pressure cooker

Cook the soaked beans in a pressure cooker for 25 to 30 minutes. Brown the meats separately, add them with the beans and enough water to cover, and pressure-cook another 20 to 25 minutes. Finish with the garlic-onion refogado as in the recipe.

Quick answers

What meats go in feijoada?

Usually pork ribs, pork loin or shoulder, bacon, smoked sausage like calabresa or paio, fresh linguiça and carne seca (dried salted beef). Pig's feet, ears and tail are traditional in feijoada completa.

What is carne seca in English?

Dried salted beef, sometimes sold as jerked beef. It must be soaked for 12 to 24 hours, changing the water several times, before cooking.
