One Family Meat Bundle, Seven Dinners: A Weekly Meal Plan That Saves Money

A family bundle saves money at the counter, but the real savings come at home, when every piece gets used and nothing ends up forgotten in the freezer. Here's a simple plan to turn one bundle into seven dinners.

How our bundles work

Our family combos put several cuts together for less than buying each piece on its own. The mix changes from week to week, so ask at the counter or check the online store for this week's bundles. The plan below uses a typical mix; swap in whatever is in yours.

A typical family bundle

  • Ground beef (carne moída)
  • Chicken thighs or leg quarters
  • Pork chops
  • Linguiça or another sausage
  • Steaks or a cut for the grill

The 30-minute prep session

  1. Portion everything into meal-size packs as soon as you get home.
  2. Marinate the chicken in garlic, lime and salt for Tuesday.
  3. Freeze what you'll cook after Wednesday: label each pack with the cut and the date.
  4. Move tomorrow's pack from the freezer to the fridge each night so it thaws safely.

Keep it safe

  • Ground beef, sausage and chicken: cook within 1 to 2 days in the fridge, or freeze.
  • Steaks and chops: cook within 3 to 5 days, or freeze.
  • Thaw in the fridge, never on the counter. Cooked leftovers keep 3 to 4 days.

Quick answers

What is a family meat bundle?

A combo of several cuts, such as ground beef, chicken, pork chops, sausage and steaks, put together so it costs less than buying each item separately. The mix at Costa's changes week to week.

How long does a meat bundle last?

With planning, a family bundle can cover a week of dinners for a family of four. Freeze what you won't cook in the first few days and thaw each pack in the fridge the night before.
