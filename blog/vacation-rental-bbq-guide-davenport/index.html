Grilling at Your Vacation Rental Near Disney: A Davenport Butcher's Guide

Every week, families fill the pool homes and villas around Davenport, ChampionsGate, and Four Corners, and most of those homes have a grill out back. A few grill nights can save a lot compared with eating every meal near the parks. Here's how to do it right.

Before you shop: check the grill

  • Gas or charcoal? Most rental homes have a propane grill. Check the tank before you buy anything: lift it, and if it feels light, it's close to empty. Some communities don't allow charcoal, so read your house rules.
  • Clean the grates. Heat the grill on high for 10–15 minutes with the lid closed, then scrub with a grill brush or a ball of foil held in tongs.
  • Bring or buy a few tools: long tongs, an instant-read thermometer, foil, and paper towels. Rental kitchens rarely have a meat thermometer.

Keep it cold in the Florida heat

Florida heat is hard on raw meat. The USDA says perishable food shouldn't sit out for more than 1 hour when it's above 90 °F. Bring a cooler bag for the car, head straight back to the house, and get everything into the fridge.

  • Steaks and roasts: cook within 3–5 days, or freeze.
  • Ground beef and chicken: cook within 1–2 days, or freeze.
  • Change of plans? Freeze anything you won't cook in time instead of letting it go to waste.

Grilling on a rental gas grill

  1. Preheat with the lid closed for 10–15 minutes. Rental grills are often older and run hotter on one side, so find the hot spot first.
  2. Make two zones: one burner on high, one on low. Sear on high, then move thicker cuts to the low side to finish.
  3. Use the thermometer. USDA minimums: 145 °F for steaks with a 3-minute rest, 160 °F for burgers and ground beef, 165 °F for chicken.
  4. Rest steaks for 5 minutes before slicing.
  5. Close the propane valve on the tank when you're done.

Easy upgrades that feel like a vacation

  • Churrasco night: thick picanha steaks with coarse salt, sliced thin at the table. Start with our picanha guide.
  • Five-minute chimichurri: chopped parsley, garlic, dried oregano, red wine vinegar, olive oil, salt, and a pinch of red pepper flakes.
  • Grilled pineapple brushed with butter and cinnamon for dessert.
