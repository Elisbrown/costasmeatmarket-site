Costa's Meat Market Coupons and Deals: Every Way to Save at the Counter

Looking for a Costa's Meat Market coupon? We don't hide our deals behind fine print. Here's every way to pay less for good meat at our Davenport counter, and where to find each one.

1. Join the WhatsApp VIP group

This is where our best deals show up first. Members get discount codes, weekly specials and fresh arrivals before they're posted anywhere else, and before the popular cuts sell out. You can also ask questions right in the group, like "is the picanha in today?"

Prefer fewer messages? Follow our WhatsApp channel instead. It's announcements only, no group chatter. Both are on our socials page.

2. Get 5% off for following us

Follow and engage with Costa's Meat Market on all of our socials (Instagram, TikTok and Facebook) and get 5% off your next purchase. Just show us at the counter. One coupon per customer.

3. Shop the weekly specials

Every week we feature cuts at special prices. They're announced in the WhatsApp group and channel and on our social pages. Ask at the counter too; sometimes the best deal is whatever just came in.

4. Buy a family bundle

Our family combos and BBQ bundles put several cuts together, so a week of dinners or a weekend cookout costs less than buying each piece on its own. Here's how to plan a week of meals around a bundle.

5. Order online and plan ahead

Planning saves money. Check the online store, build your order at home, and pick it up at the counter. You'll buy what you need instead of grabbing extras on an empty stomach.

6. Choose cuts that give more for less

Some of the tastiest cuts cost less than steak: chuck, chicken thighs, pork shoulder, ground beef, and our seasoned picadinho. Our guide to budget-friendly cuts shows how to cook each one.

Good to knowCodes and specials change from week to week, and we only announce them through our own channels. If you see a "Costa's coupon code" on a random coupon website, it didn't come from us; check the WhatsApp group instead.

Where to find us

Costa's Meat Market, 2169 Davenport Blvd, Davenport, FL 33837 (Webb's Town Center), near Haines City and ChampionsGate. Call (863) 422-2313.
