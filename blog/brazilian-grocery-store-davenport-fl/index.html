Brazilian Groceries in Davenport, FL: What to Look For (and What to Make with It)

Missing the flavors of home? Alongside our butcher counter, Costa's carries Brazilian and Latin pantry staples, so you can pick up the picanha and the farofa in one stop. Here's what to look for and what to make with it.

Breakfast and bakery

  • Frozen pão francês: the classic Brazilian roll, baked at home in minutes. Brands like Panebras are a favorite.
  • Polvilho and pão de queijo mix: for real cheese bread. See our pão de queijo recipe.
  • Jams and honey: fruit preserves and imported honey for your toast.

Pantry staples

  • Black beans (brands like Camil) for everyday feijão and feijoada.
  • Brazilian rice (brands like Tio João).
  • Coarse churrasco salt (sal grosso), the only seasoning a picanha needs.
  • Seasonings and spices for your tempero.
  • Charcoal for the grill.

Frozen fruit pulp

Frozen fruit pulps like acerola make fresh Brazilian-style juice in seconds: blend one packet with water and sugar to taste. They're also great in smoothies.

Sweets and treats

Brazilian chocolates such as Ouro Branco and Baton, and gummy candies from Fini, are the treats kids (and adults) ask for.

Good to knowOur grocery selection rotates, and imported items can sell out. If you're coming for something specific, call (863) 422-2313 or check the online store first.

Make it a one-stop shop

Pick up the groceries with your meat: picanha and sal grosso for Sunday, black beans and pork for feijoada, or pão francês and our house-made linguiça for a quick pão com linguiça. Browse our ready-to-cook seasoned meats too.
