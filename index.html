About Our Butcher Counter

Costa's Meat Market is a full-service meat market and specialty grocer serving Davenport, Haines City, Kissimmee, and surrounding Central Florida communities.

  • Premium Beef Cuts: Traditional Picanha (Top Sirloin Cap), Churrasco (Skirt Steak), Ribeye, NY Strip, Short Ribs, and Fresh Ground Chuck.
  • Pork & Poultry: Fresh pork chops, pork belly (toucinho/panceta), whole chickens, wings, and house-seasoned cuts.
  • Family Combos: Weekly barbecue meat bundles designed for families and weekend grillers.
  • WhatsApp VIP Drops: First access to discount codes, weekly specials, and fresh arrivals before they sell out.
