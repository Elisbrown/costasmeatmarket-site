About Our Butcher Counter

Costa's Meat Market is a full-service meat market and specialty grocer serving Davenport, Haines City, ChampionsGate, Four Corners, Poinciana, Kissimmee, Winter Haven, and surrounding Central Florida communities.

  • Premium Beef Cuts: Traditional Picanha (Top Sirloin Cap), Churrasco (Skirt Steak), Ribeye, NY Strip, Maminha (Tri-Tip), Alcatra, Short Ribs, and Fresh Ground Chuck.
  • Pork & Poultry: Fresh pork chops, pork belly (toucinho/panceta), whole chickens, wings, and house-seasoned cuts.
  • Ready to Cook: House-made linguiça, seasoned picadinho, marinated pork and seasoned chicken wings.
  • Family Combos: Weekly barbecue meat bundles designed for families and weekend grillers.
  • Latin & Brazilian Cuts and Groceries: Ask for your cut by the name you know from home, and pick up Brazilian and Latin pantry staples while you're here.
  • WhatsApp VIP Drops: First access to discount codes, weekly specials, and fresh arrivals before they sell out.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where is Costa's Meat Market?

We're at 2169 Davenport Blvd, Davenport, FL 33837, in Webb's Town Center. Tap Get Directions above for navigation, or call (863) 422-2313.

Is Costa's Meat Market near Haines City?

Yes. Davenport borders Haines City, so we're a short drive away for customers in Haines City, ChampionsGate, Four Corners and Poinciana.

Can I order online?

Yes. Browse our online store and place your order for pickup at the counter.

Do you do custom cuts and special orders?

Yes. Ask at the counter or call ahead for custom thickness, trimming, marinades, and special orders.

Do you carry picanha and Brazilian or Latin cuts?

Yes. Picanha is one of our specialties, along with Latin and Brazilian favorites. Availability changes daily, so call or check our WhatsApp channel for today's arrivals.

How do I get deals?

Join our WhatsApp VIP group for discount codes, weekly specials, and fresh arrivals. Follow and engage with us on all of our socials and get 5% off your next purchase. Just show us at the counter (one coupon per customer).

What payment methods do you accept?

Cash, credit cards, and debit cards.
